Generate a digital will as a PDF whenever you like: every account and its ending, your executors, and a personal message in your own words. Your executors can download it once they have access.

What you should know.

  • You decide who sees what, and when.Executors see nothing until you grant access or your check-ins stop. You can revoke access at any time.
  • Executors see the sign-ins you leave.That's the point: once they have access, they can open each account. Only add what you're comfortable handing over.
  • Your own login is protected.Account passwords for DIGIPASS itself are hashed, and invite links for executors expire after 24 hours.
  • It's free.Create an account, add your first few accounts, and come back whenever your life changes.
