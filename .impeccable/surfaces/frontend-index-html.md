# Landing page (Persuade)

Scope: frontend/index.html. Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led. Structure chosen by the user on the decision page: "The handover, played" (sketch .impeccable/mocks/decision/handover.html). Palette pinned by PRODUCT.md.

Audience: adults thinking about what happens to their accounts after they die; secondary: executors arriving from an invite. Action: Start free (register); executors sign in.

Truth constraints: no encryption-at-rest or end-to-end claims (passwords are stored readable and shown to executors once access is granted); the switch grants access, it does not email executors; no testimonials, user counts, or prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the product's own story played by its real parts: account cards drop into the fate the owner chose, the check-in pulse slows and stops, and the same cards travel to an executor's board and get taken care of. Refuses the category template of a split hero, emoji feature cards and a 3-step row.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest #1b3a2d hero ground with cream type; the sand board (#ece3d2) and white cards from the app itself, tag pills in sage and sand; DM Sans heavy and tight for display, regular for body; one sage pulse as the only glow.

STORY: Visitors see what DIGIPASS does in seconds (choose an ending per account), understand the trigger (check-ins), see the handover with guidance, and start free; executors find their door.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav (logo, How it works, For executors, Sign in, Start free). Left, a two-line display headline (~72px) "Every account gets the ending you choose." Right, a short paragraph and Start free. Below, full-width sand board with three fate columns; account cards drop into them on load, one by one.

FORM: User-locked dealt structure "The handover, played", position 1 of 7 on my ranked list. seed:eccb2179df9b9a44

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: a sticky "handover" stage scrubbed by scroll in which the same card elements move (FLIP) from the owner's fate columns, through a pausing heartbeat, into the executor's Not started / In progress / Taken care of columns. Reduced motion shows each state statically.
